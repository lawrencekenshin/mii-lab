How to read it
Right now

Scared = a stock whose TET mood reading sits in its washed-out zone. Today’s fund members, back in time.

Percentile = how a reading ranks against finished candles of the same size since 2021 (or since 2024); an unfinished candle is ranked “so far”. Dashed purple lines = the 90th, 95th and 99th percentile levels since 2021. Recent peak = the highest reading of the last few weeks (or candles), the latest one included.

Method: Trend Emotion Timing (TET) by Dr. Oliver Reiss (NAAIM 2025). This is an independent re-implementation, not affiliated with or endorsed by the author or NAAIM. Alarm line = a high reading for that candle size by its own history.

Prices: daily closes; during the session the fear readings include today’s open candle (about 15 minutes delayed), while the prices and their 1D / 1M / off-high moves stay at the last close. No cookies, no tracking.

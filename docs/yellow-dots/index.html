Yellow dot — the Washout indicator's yellow dot (the same dot as on the Eagle Eye charts): the candle on which a stock is marked washed out after a deep sell-off. D · W · 2W · M = daily, weekly, 2-week and monthly candles. A filled chip = a dot on the last finished candle; an outlined chip = a dot 1–2 candles ago.

Business — a 0–100 score of the business: revenue and earnings growth, PEG, free-cash-flow growth and margin and last quarter's revenue, each ranked against the S&P 500 + Nasdaq-100 and blended. The board lists names at 50+ and opens on 60+; mortgage REITs are left out.

Calls : puts held — open call contracts against open put contracts over every strike and expiry at the latest settle. Top / bottom 10% = among the most call-heavy / put-heavy tenth of all stocks with a real options book.

New positions — call and put contracts added over the last week's settles, with the side the Skew Tracker reads for each window (1D · 1W · 2W · 1M: green calls, red puts, grey no clear side); a list tag is the name's rank on the Skew Tracker's call or put list.

King GEX — the King GEX Tracker's king node for the name (the strike with the most call gamma), how strong it is against every tracked name, and how many sessions it has held. FRESH = open interest piling up at the king contract over the last week of settles, with a large part of it new. PUTS > KING = a put-heavy cluster carries more gamma than the king.

From high — the last close against the 52-week high. KDJ — the state on each candle size: GC = golden cross, coil, up, down.

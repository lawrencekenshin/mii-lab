Model line changes

Model line — the anchored analog. The anchor is the current cycle low: the lowest close of about the past year, reached after a correction and not undercut since, with price now well above it. The path since that low is matched against the path after every past cycle low of the Nasdaq-100 (1985+) and the S&P 500 (1950+). A past cycle may run faster or slower and bigger or smaller than this one; the match score (fit) is the correlation of the two paths, with one cycle per historical episode. The best match is locked and its projection frozen; it stays the model line until a new cycle low, or until price has stayed well away from it for about a week.

Other cycles — the next nine matches, each projected the same way. Analog spread — the 10–90% spread of all ten, weighted by match score. Recent-shape matches — the last six months or so of QQQ against every stretch of the same length of the Nasdaq-100.

Range — 50% and 80% ranges from today's VXN (the Nasdaq-100 volatility index), widening with time, with a small upward drift. How often real moves landed inside them is in the Record.

Cycle phase — the four stages of a market cycle (Weinstein's stages, the same four as Wyckoff's phases), read from the long-term average and its recent slope. Markup = price above a rising average; Markdown = below a falling one; otherwise Distribution after Markup, Accumulation after Markdown.

Technical checks — plain readings of QQQ's own bars and of this board's own ranges. Averages: QQQ against its 20-day, 50-day and 200-day averages of daily closes, whether each is rising or falling, and their order (stacked up = the shorter averages above the longer ones). KDJ: the KDJ oscillator on weekly, 2-week and monthly bars, the current bar read as if it closed today. A golden cross is K crossing above D, a death cross K crossing below it; coiling = K still on the other side of D but turned toward it and closing in. J washed out = J has dropped through the floor of its scale, stretched = through the ceiling; D's low / high zone = near the bottom / top of its range. A recent golden cross "came out of a washout" when J or D was there going in; a recent death cross "came off a high" when J was stretched or D in its high zone going in. VXN level: today's VXN against every session of the past year and since 2013; calm = among the lowest fifth of the past year, elevated = the top fifth, very high = the top twentieth. Ranges made a month ago: where today's price sits in the VXN range and the analog spread this board drew a month ago. Swings: the latest daily high (low) that no session within about a week on either side went above (below), from completed sessions; the 1-year high is the highest price of the past year.

Odds — every session since 2013 with VXN close to today's level, and what QQQ did next.

Record — the model line rebuilt every Friday since January 2025 and the forecasts saved after each close, each made only from data known that day and scored against what QQQ did after; the walk-forward test reruns the model over 2013 → now the same way. Gap — the average distance between a line and the real closes after it was made; flat-line gap — the same for a flat line at that day's close.

Prices: daily bars of QQQ, the Nasdaq-100, the S&P 500 and VXN. Rebuilt after each close; today's so-far candle updates during US hours.

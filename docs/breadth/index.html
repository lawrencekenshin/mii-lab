The numbers as a table

Breadth = the share of today’s index members whose close is above its 20-, 50-, 100- or 200-day average. Earlier readings can run a little higher than the official indices, because names that later left an index are not counted.

Washout = the 50-day line drops to a deeply washed-out level after it had recovered since the previous washout. Hollow ring = the deepest close of a washout that had not reset, when it came clearly later and lower. The KDJ blocks’ weekly, 2-week and monthly candles use the breadth of their last trading day.

Nasdaq-100 and Russell 2000: the same lines on today’s members of each index, updated after each close; the S&P 500 also updates during the session. Today’s Russell 2000 list holds many young companies, so its early years count fewer, surviving names. The Russell 1000 is left out: it moves with the S&P 500 almost exactly.

Informational only. Not investment advice. Nothing here says when to buy or sell.

Percentile = where a reading ranks among past candles of the same size since 2013 (or since 2021); an open candle is ranked “so far”. Low percentile = few stocks above their average, rare.

Derived values only (counts of stocks; SPY, QQQ and IWM closes). Prices about 15 minutes delayed during the session. No cookies, no tracking.

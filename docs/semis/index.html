      Signals: what each has meant before
        Taiwan data in full

        Several dozen series measured the same way: company and industry revenue (MOPS), export orders (MOEA), customs exports (MOF), foreign flows, the TSM ADR premium and TAIEX. Growth = last 3 months vs the same 3 months a year earlier.

        Findings

        How far behind price

        Price was about 3.3 months ahead of what you could read in TWSE semis and TSMC, the same as WSTS. Memory, TPEx semis and IC exports run about 1 month closer. The AI-server chain (ODMs, Quanta, Wiwynn, King Slide) lags 6–12 months. Japan tools, US orders and Micron sit 5.7–7.2 months behind.

        2021 to now

        Only TWSE semis (about +8 months) and TSMC (+11) give a stable reading, and that is one cycle's phase, not a lead estimate. For the other series the 2021+ lead or lag cannot be pinned down: the plausible range is too wide to use.

        At the lows

        2025: no TWSE semis, TSMC, ODM, export-order or customs series went negative. Only memory did (basket −8%, Nanya −28%), and it turned up 2 days after the low: one case. 2026: nothing turned down. The turn-ups before the low (ASPEED, TSMC and IC exports on Feb 10) were followed by another 10% fall. Optoelectronics bottomed in late 2025, but it is not an AI-cycle series.

        Turn signals vs a fair test

        Pooled into independent episodes, Taiwan trough-turn and cross-above-zero signals beat the fair benchmark only once each, in the late-2023 confirmation cluster (SMH already 45%+ off the 2022 low), and never in 2013-20.

        Foreign investors

        They sold into every low (NT$656bn over the 13 weeks to the 2026 low). Their net buying turned positive with SMH already 24–28% off the low, and after the 2026 low they kept selling on balance (−NT$257bn over 13 weeks) while SMH rose about 85%. The cross-above-zero signal passes 2021-now but fails 2013-20.

        TSM ADR premium

        Its washouts showed up at real lows and at failed bounces alike (Mar and Jul 2022). It is noisy: the two closes are 14 hours apart, so US-only news moves it by up to 18 points in a day. At the study's Oct 1 reading it was +16.6%: 87th percentile since 2013, 48th of the last 3 years (study note; it is not updated here).

        TAIEX and the Taiwan dollar

        None of the weekly lead tests pass. SMH appears to lead Taiwan by one week only because Taiwan's Monday trades on SMH's Friday: a time-zone overlap, not a signal. When the study was written, TAIEX had closed at a record 48,476 on Oct 2 (+84% over 12 months) while SMH sat 7.6% below its high. The TWD moved with the 2022 and 2025 lows but not consistently across eras.

        A fix to the earlier study

        The first study's Taiwan semis series used the MOPS page total. From Apr 2018 to Apr 2019 that total counted ASE's new holding company with a year-ago value of 0, inflating growth by up to 21 points. Counted the same companies both years, Dec 2018 read +5% (not +26%), fell to −9% in Apr 2019 and turned up on 2019-07-10 (SMH +38%). This page uses the corrected series.

        Small date caveat: a few MOPS signal dates fall on a Taiwan holiday, when the real filing deadline rolled a few days later. No headline result depends on them.

        Korea data in full

        Six series measured the same way as the Taiwan card: customs chip exports (KCS 10-day, 20-day and final prints), the trade ministry's monthly memory and system-chip exports, SK Hynix and Samsung against SMH (in dollars), and the won. Korea items use Seoul dates; 09:00 Seoul is 20:00 ET the previous day (19:00 in winter), before the US open. System-chip exports to Dec 2025 come from UN Comtrade.

        Findings

        Study notes from the Korea study of Oct 3, 2026 (2021-now first, 2013-20 for consistency). The numbers in these notes do not update; the readings below them do.

        How early, how good

        The 20-day print is public about 9 days before month end, 10 days before the full-month first print and 25 days before the final. In 2021-now it matched the final at r 0.98 (median gap 6 points, same sign in 97% of months) and called whether the month would beat the previous one 73% of the time (79% when the final moved by more than 5 points); 2016-20 was similar (r 0.96, median gap 5 points, same sign 89%, direction 84%). The 10-day print is 10 days earlier again but noisier (2021-now r 0.95, median gap 10 points), because holidays shift the working days.

        How far behind price

        SMH's 12-month return lines up best with the 20-day print 3 months later, in 2021-now and in 2016-20 alike; SK Hynix's 3–4 months, Micron's 1 month. Month by month, past SMH moves explain later changes in the print (r 0.37) better than the print's acceleration explains SMH's next 3 months (r 0.18). Memory exports sit about 3 months behind SMH and 1 behind Micron; system chips about 8 behind SMH.

        At the lows

        2022: the 20-day print read +3% at the low (Oct 1–10: −21%, out 3 days before it), bottomed at −45% in March 2023 (+5 months); its trough-turn signal fired a year after the low, with SMH already +67%. 2025: it had slowed to +12% and bottomed in the low month (+11% on Apr 21); its acceleration turn-up came Jul 21 with SMH +61%. 2026: +164% and accelerating at the low, so nothing to turn. Memory exports read −18%, +5% and +147% at the three lows. 2013-20: in Dec 2018 the print first turned negative (−10%) one session before the low and kept falling to −29% in Jan 2019; in Feb 2020 it read +15% and rising, with SMH still to fall 30% to its Covid low.

        The June 2026 top and the July drop

        The June 20-day print came out the morning of SMH's Jun 22 top: +188%, a little under May's +202%. Through the −17.6% July the prints read +193% (Jul 13) and +181% (Jul 21), and memory exports kept accelerating (3-month +250% to +271%). SK Hynix peaked with SMH and fell 52% in dollars to its Jul 30 low (SMH −25%, Micron −39%). No Korean series warned.

        Turn signals vs a fair test

        Its acceleration turn-ups did not beat the fair benchmark in 2021-now (1 of 3; −8 points after about 3 months) but did in 2016-20 (2 of 2). The trough-turn (Oct 2023, SMH +67% off the low) and cross-above-zero (Dec 2023, +104%) are single 2021+ episodes; the Feb 2020 cross-above-zero came before a further 30% fall. Nothing passes in both eras, and the print's level and acceleration had no forecasting value out of sample. Memory exports: one 2021+ cross-above-zero win (Jan 2024); acceleration signals were false in 3 of 6 fires in 2013-20.

        Release days

        SMH against SPY did not move with the surprise on KCS release days (r −0.003 over 138 releases since 2021), and SK Hynix's own move on those days was no larger than on other days (2.3% on average, both). The print has not been news to prices.

        SK Hynix, Samsung, KOSPI and the won

        SK Hynix and Samsung move with SMH and Micron within the same week (no weekly lead in either era; SMH's Friday trades into Korea's Monday, a time-zone overlap, not a lead). Their strength against SMH did not forecast SMH over the next 3 months or so (exploratory; no forecasting value found). In dollars, SK Hynix bottomed within 2 sessions of SMH at 3 of 4 lows in 2021-now and 2 of 3 in 2016-20, and 2–3 weeks earlier in 2016 and 2022 (Micron too in 2022); Samsung at 3 of 4 and 1 of 3. The March 2026 low was a dip inside a rise for both (+43% and +49% over the 3 months before it). Too few and too irregular to use as a lead. KOSPI's move is confirmation, and memory-vs-logic baskets did not pass the race's bar.

        A late-cycle reading, not a timing signal

        Very high Korea memory export growth has been followed by weaker SMH 12-month returns in both eras (r −0.46 in 2021-now, −0.57 in 2014-20; about 5–7 independent years each, picked from many correlations). The +311% reading of Oct 1 is far outside that sample. Read it as caution about the cycle's stage, not as a date.

        Not shown

        Korea's DRAM and flash price indexes (Bank of Korea ECOS) need a free API key, which has not been set up. Company filing times (OpenDART) also need a key; note only. The Korea Exchange data portal's terms restrict automated use, so Korean foreign-investor flows are not shown.

        Memory prices vs MU and SMH RE-CHECKED

        Public DRAM and NAND contract-price calls (TrendForce press headlines, 2016–2026), dated by the day they became public, tested against Micron and SMH turns. Re-checked independently on Oct 3, 2026 (11 key releases checked one by one); the corrections are applied below.

        • The calls mostly came after the turns. The first conventional-DRAM "prices turn up" call came 2–12 months after Micron's lows, with MU already up 22–85% (in 2025 on Jul 7, 94 days after the low; an earlier Apr 1 figure included HBM).         • Latest call (Sep 30): Q4 conventional DRAM +10–15% q/q, the third slowdown in a row (Q1 was called at +90–95% on Feb 2 and reported at +93–98% on Mar 31); NAND +15–20%, speeding up again. In 2018 and 2024 Micron topped before the first negative call; in 2021 the call came first.

        The verdict, track by track

        Price and breadth signals

        Rules fixed in advance: market washouts, chip breadth, trend, relative strength, momentum

        What fired, when
        The S&P 500 breadth washout day fired 20, 2 and 6 sessions before the 2022, 2025 and 2026 lows. After the 2022 one, SMH fell another 15%. The S&P KDJ meter's STRONG reading came within 11% of all three lows. Trend, relative-strength and momentum signals came 32–68% above the lows in 2022 and 2025, and never fired in 2026 because the uptrend never broke.
        False alarms
        The washout day's false-alarm rate is the same as a random day's: 45% vs 43%. The KDJ meter also fired in Jan 2025, after which SMH fell 27% more. The breadth-thrust rule's Jul-2022 and Jan-2025 false starts cost 25–31%.
        Edge vs a fair test
        None. Returns after a washout match the fair benchmark (p 0.2–0.4). Random dates land "early at all three lows" only 0.7–2.6% of the time, so the washouts did locate the lows in time; they just added no return. Nothing survives a multiple-testing correction.

        Told you when a market washout was happening, not whether the drop was over.

        Chart indicators on SMH

        KDJ on 1-week, 2-week and 1-month candles; MACD-V; OBV-MACD; Washout dots

        What fired, when (the 2026 low)
        Weekly KDJ washout on Mar 27, one session before the low (SMH fell another 3%). Weekly coil-up Apr 2 (+8% off the low). Weekly golden cross Apr 10 (+21%). OBV-MACD flip Apr 17 (+28%). 2-week golden cross and MACD-V momentum-on Apr 24 (+40%). Monthly golden cross Apr 30 (+40%). Washout dots and MACD-V "cross up from LOW" never fired: weekly MACD-V never dropped below +82.
        False alarms
        The weekly golden cross also came after 5 of 6 false starts, and 4 of those 5 then broke the low. OBV flips came after 6 of 6 real starts and 6 of 6 false starts.
        Edge vs a fair test
        None that holds up. The weekly KDJ washout's single "pass" is about what chance alone produces, and it holds at only one setting, and its 2021+ edge is the 2025 and 2026 rallies themselves. From 2021 on, 46% of all SMH days were followed by a +20% gain within 6 months anyway.

        Fired within about 2 weeks of every low, and the same way at bounces that failed.

        Free chip data

        WSTS chip sales, Korea and Japan exports, US output and orders, US semis revenue, Micron margin, hyperscaler capex

        What fired, when
        Price led the data by about 3–7 months. At the 2022 low the data bottomed 5–11 months later, and the pre-registered turn signals came 12–17 months late, with SMH already +66% to +160%. At the 2025 low no series in this set went negative (Korea chip exports, 3-month total from UN Comtrade, only slowed to +1.6%, bottoming in the low month). At the 2026 low only Japan chip-tool exports were negative (−6%). Taiwan memory did turn negative in 2025 (see Taiwan).
        False alarms
        Earlier re-accelerations were false starts: Nov 2021 to Feb 2022 (SMH then fell another 35–43%) and Feb 2025 (another 28%).
        Edge vs a fair test
        "This crash is not the chip cycle" was the right read in 2025 and 2026, but it cannot be scored: every deep SMH drop since 2013 recovered anyway. Late confirmation still beat the fair test in 2016, 2019-20 and 2023, after a large part of the move. US data was revised later by up to 12 points.

        Confirmed the rallies; never forecast them.

        Taiwan data

        MOPS company revenue, MOEA export orders, MOF customs exports, foreign flows, the TSM ADR premium, TAIEX

        What fired, when
        Faster, not earlier. TWSE semis and TSMC sit about 3.3 months behind price, the same as WSTS. Memory, TPEx semis and IC exports are about 1 month closer. At the 2025 low only memory had turned down (basket −8%, Nanya −28%), and it turned up 2 days after the low (one case). Before the 2026 low the first turn-ups (Feb 10) were followed by another 10% fall.
        False alarms
        Similar turn-ups fired at 2–5 of the 6 false-start lows for most series. Twelve Taiwan acceleration signals in Jan–Mar 2025 came before a further 16–28% SMH fall.
        Edge vs a fair test
        Pooled into independent episodes, Taiwan turn signals beat the fair test only in the late-2023 cluster, with SMH already +45% off the 2022 low, and never in 2013-20.

        The fastest confirmation available; still a confirmation.

        Catalysts and news

        Macro shocks, company prints, estimates, "who called it"

        Why the lows happened
        Only the 2026 low was a purely outside shock (the Iran war and oil). 2022 was a real chip bust plus macro. In 2025, AI-capex scares (DeepSeek, Microsoft lease cuts) did about two-thirds of the drop and tariffs the rest.
        What turned it
        2022: the low came first; soft CPI arrived 18 sessions later with SMH already +12%. 2025: the Apr 9 tariff pause, a +17% day that came before anyone could act on the announcement. 2026: the Mar 31 war-exit headline, which was the third de-escalation headline; the first two (Mar 9, Mar 23) were each followed by another 7–8% drop.
        Hard evidence, measured from the next open
        AI-demand evidence lagged price only in 2022. In 2025-26 it was public 8–64 sessions before the low and SMH still fell another 8–31%. Waiting for hard evidence: 2022 ×4.38 vs ×7.06 from the low; 2025 ×2.40 vs ×3.32 (both to Sep 21, 2026); 2026 +34% vs +85% to the June peak. "Estimates up, price down" happened 3 times in the AI era (Sep 2023, Sep 2024, Mar 2026): 2 rallies after a further 3–5% dip, 1 failure.

        3 episodes (arguably 2): case studies, not statistics.

        Bottom line. In real time you could see two things. One was a market-wide washout: it located the lows in time but gave no return edge. The other, in 2025 and 2026 only, was a chip business that was not rolling over: right both times but impossible to score. In 2022 the chip business really was busting. Everything else either confirmed the turn after it started or fired the same way at bounces that failed. No signal survives multiple testing.

        AI build-out names: what the price assumes

        The chips, networking, hardware and power names from a "next NVIDIA / AMD / Palantir" search, with the tiers after an independent re-check. Prices as of the Oct 1, 2026 close.

        The yardstick. A reverse valuation: the yearly revenue growth over the next decade that today's price already assumes ("price needs"). Read every figure as a rough band, not a point.

        What history says about this kind of search

        Picking fast growers with high margins beat the average stock only while growth stocks were in favour. 2012-16 picks tripled in 5 years 31.7% of the time vs 15.7% for all stocks (about 1.4× same-sector peers). 2017-20 picks did no better than average, and 2021-22 picks are within noise (3-year hit rate 17.6% vs 16.6%, about one window). Adding "accelerating" or "strong momentum" made it worse after 2016. Paying the highest price-to-sales multiples went bust about half the time (exploratory).

        The famous winners failed the screen

        At their exact pre-run lows, NVIDIA (2015-16), AMD (2016), Palantir (2022), AppLovin, Tesla, Super Micro, Vertiv, Enphase and Netflix all failed the fast-grower screen: they were slow, beaten-down and cheap. (Those dates are hindsight lows; NVIDIA did pass the screen at the end of 2017, 2018, 2021 and 2022.)

        Base rates

        Only about 4% of US stocks created all the net wealth from 1926 to 2016. Sustaining 25%+ real growth for 10 years happened for about 1% of firms starting at $2–3B of sales and 0% above $25B. Even the winners fell 32.5% on average within their best decade.

        Left out or not researched

        Memory (Micron, SanDisk) is excluded: a pricing super-cycle at peak margins. ASML passed the screen but was not researched (the price needs about 19–20%/yr). Taiwan- and Japan-listed AI suppliers were outside the US-only universe.
        Method & caveats
        • Rules first. Each track wrote its rules down before scoring; for the price-signal track the timing could not be verified from file times, and windows added later are labelled post hoc. A second, independent run rebuilt the four main tracks, the Taiwan layer and the hardware trade and memory studies with its own code and corrected the claims that did not hold; this page uses the corrected versions. The GPU price record is a first reading and has not been tested.
        • Few episodes. 3 rally starts in 2021-now (arguably 2, since the 2026 low sits inside the 2025 cycle) and 3 in 2013-20 (Feb 2016, Dec 2018, Mar 2020). These are case studies. 2021-now is about one cycle.
        • The fair test. Every signal's later SMH return is compared with a fair benchmark, not with zero. A big drop alone explains most rebounds: every deep SMH drop since 2013 recovered.
        • Multiple testing. Hundreds of combinations were tested. After correcting for that, nothing survives.
        • As known on each day. Data series are dated by the day they became public. MOF, MOEA and MOPS values are today's versions, not first prints; US data was revised by up to 12 points.
        • Survivorship. The chip-breadth universe, the S&P KDJ meter and the MOPS pages use today's companies; only the S&P 500 breadth line is free of it. The AI baskets (ODMs, ASPEED, King Slide, AI chip/IP) were picked today because they are winners.
        • Prices. SMH and SPY adjusted closes (Yahoo Finance daily bars; closed sessions only). The chart indicators use split-adjusted (not dividend-adjusted) prices, as on a TradingView chart.
        • Gaps. Korea chip exports via UN Comtrade stop at Dec 2025 (Korea memory exports are extended with the trade ministry's 2026 releases). TSMC's and SEAJ's own sites refused automated access and were not worked around. The MOEA open-data file runs one release behind. The hardware trade and memory studies were re-checked on Oct 3, 2026; the GPU price record is a single first reading.
        • Percentiles on this page. Percentile = where a reading ranks in the same series' own history since 2013 (the Korea 20-day print: since 2016). Dashed purple lines mark rare levels of that history; the Korea price panels show both tails. Recent peak = the highest recent reading.

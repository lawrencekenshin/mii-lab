Fetching the latest numbers…

Inflation data: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (CPI) and U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (PCE), retrieved from FRED and ALFRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (CPIAUCSL, CPILFESL, CPIAUCNS, CPILFENS, PCEPI, PCEPILFE). Treasury yields: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, retrieved from FRED (DGS2, DGS10, DFII10). The economic-data model's other inputs are FRED series too (BLS, Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago, U.S. Energy Information Administration).

Inflation nowcasts: Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland. Supply- and demand-driven PCE inflation: Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco (Shapiro). Multivariate Core Trend: Federal Reserve Bank of New York. Risk-free rate: Kenneth R. French Data Library. Bond-stock beta method: Campbell, Pflueger and Viceira (2026), Annual Review of Financial Economics; Pflueger (2025), Journal of Financial Economics.

SPY and QQQ appear only as returns (with dividends) and as this page's own model results. Release dates: BLS and BEA published schedules. Times are New York time.

KDJ of the fear meter describes the swing in fear.

The numbers as a table

Scared = a stock whose TET mood reading sits in its washed-out zone. Today’s members, back in time.

Percentile = how a reading ranks against finished candles of the same size since 2013 (or since 2021); an unfinished candle is ranked “so far”. Dashed purple lines = the 90th, 95th and 99th percentile levels since 2013. Recent peak = the highest reading of the last few weeks (or candles), the latest one included.

S&P 500 line = the live S&P 500 fear meter (the Fear meter tab), the same TET mood rule over the S&P 500 stocks.

Method: Trend Emotion Timing (TET) by Dr. Oliver Reiss (NAAIM 2025). This is an independent re-implementation, not affiliated with or endorsed by the author or NAAIM. Alarm line = a high reading for that candle size by its own history.

Prices: daily closing bars; during the session the open candle comes from a ~15-min delayed quote. No cookies, no tracking.

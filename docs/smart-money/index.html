Smart Money Flow — a running total of each session's move from after the open to the close: the overnight gap and the opening stretch are left out. Recipe published by WallStreetCourier (the Smart Money Flow Index). History from 2013.

So far — a candle that hasn't closed yet (this week, these two weeks, this month, and today during US hours) is drawn dashed, and its numbers are marked so far. It can still change; divergences and the after-tables use closed candles only.

Divergence — two back-to-back closing lows, the second lower while the flow line is higher (bullish), or two back-to-back closing highs, the second higher while the flow line is lower (bearish). A low or high only counts once later candles confirm it, so the arrow is dated on the confirming candle.

KDJ of the flow line. Dots mark K crossing D: green up, red down, grey while the candle is still open. Weekly, 2-week and monthly candles use the flow line's high, low and close inside the candle.

After — the index move 1, 3 and 6 months after the signal day's close, the worst close within 3 months, and the same index on every day of the same history for comparison.
